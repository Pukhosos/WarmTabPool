Warm Tab Pool

Configure reusable pools of preloaded pages.

Settings

Warm tabs stay non-discardable while pooled. Normal Firefox discarding is restored after a tab is taken.

Active pools

Type shortcuts as text, for example Ctrl+Shift+1 or F13; do not press the key combination to edit the shortcut field. Leave blank for no shortcut. Total number of active pools must not exceed 32.

No groups are active.

Pool groups

Groups store separate pool layouts and shortcuts.
