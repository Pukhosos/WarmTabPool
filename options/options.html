Warm Tab Pool

Configure up to 12 independent pools. Each pool keeps its target page preloaded in background tabs.

Background behavior

Warm tabs are always marked non-discardable while they are in a pool. Once taken, normal Firefox discarding behavior is restored.

Pools

Shortcut values must be typed as text into the field, for example Ctrl+Shift+1, Alt+F2, or F13. Do not focus the field and press the shortcut combination itself. Leave blank for no shortcut.

Pool groups

A group stores one complete 12-pool set, including its shortcuts. Load a group to make that set active in Pools and in the popup. The Default group is always available and cannot be deleted.
